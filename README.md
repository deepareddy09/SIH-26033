## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

We propose FarmConnect, an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

1. Direct Marketplace: Allows farmers to sell their products without unnecessary middlemen.
2. Fair Pricing: Provides transparent pricing for farmers and affordable products for consumers.
3. Smart Logistics: Helps arrange transportation and delivery of agricultural products.
4. AI Demand Forecasting: Predicts product demand and helps farmers plan their supply.
5. Route Optimization: Finds efficient delivery routes to reduce transportation costs.

The platform aims to create a transparent, affordable and efficient agricultural supply chain.
## Technical Approach

### Technologies Used
1. Frontend: HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and JavaScript.
2. Backend: Python and Django.
3. Database: SQLite.
4. AI/ML: Python libraries for demand forecasting.
5. Maps: Mapping services for route optimization.

### Implementation Methodology
1. Develop a user-friendly website for farmers and consumers.
2. Create registration and login features.
3. Build product listing and ordering modules.
4. Integrate AI-based demand forecasting.
5. Implement logistics and route optimization.
6. Test the application and improve its performance.

## Feasibility and Viability

### 1. Feasibility of the Idea
The platform can be developed using existing web technologies, databases and AI tools.

### 2. Potential Challenges and Risks
- Farmers may have limited digital knowledge.
- Internet connectivity may be poor in rural areas.
- Transportation and delivery may be challenging.
- Maintaining accurate market prices.

### 3. Strategies to Overcome Challenges
- Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.
- Support regional languages.
- Use reliable logistics partners.
- Update prices regularly.
- Provide training and support to farmers.
## Impact and Benefits

### 1. Impact on Target Audience
- Farmers receive better prices for their products.
- Consumers get fresh products at affordable prices.
- Bulk buyers can directly connect with farmers.

### 2. Benefits of the Solution
- Social: Improves farmers' income and supports rural communities.
- Economic: Reduces middlemen and supply chain costs.
- Environmental: Reduces food wastage and unnecessary transportation.

### 3. Long-Term Impact
- Promotes digital agriculture.
- Improves market access for farmers.
- Creates a transparent and efficient agricultural supply chain.
